    Learning with EduArcade

    EduArcade is an AI-powered adaptive learning engine that turns mistake-driven retrieval practice into an arcade game. A student searches for a topic, receives a short AI-generated lesson broken into key concepts, and then reinforces those concepts through Pac-Man gameplay. As the student encounters ghosts, they answer questions related to what they learned.

    The program is helpful because it turns passive studying into active practice. Instead of simply reading information, students have to recall and apply concepts while playing. The system also tracks performance at the individual concept level, so it can identify areas where a student is struggling, explain mistakes, and show how their understanding changes over time.

    PAC-MAN

    Use the arrow keys or WASD to move and avoid the ghosts. If a ghost catches you, answer a question to keep learning. Eat a Power Pellet to turn ghosts blue, then eat them for points.
